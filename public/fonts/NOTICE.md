# Reading fonts

Google Fonts families (Literata, Arimo, Atkinson Hyperlegible, Lora, Source Serif 4) are self-hosted from their Fontsource npm packages. Each package includes its license.

OpenDyslexic is distributed separately under the included SIL Open Font License, from https://forge.hackers.town/antijingoist/opendyslexic at revision `48218028cc8bd9f4f244cd4ce4049e6c879d4d1a`. Font files are unmodified.
